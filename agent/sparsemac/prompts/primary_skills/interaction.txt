Primary skill: interaction.
Focus on interaction evidence: contact, causality, force transfer, reciprocal/cooperative action, response, and resulting state.
Required participants/tools may be checked only as part of the interaction chain.
Temporal order matters: cause/contact/action should precede the response/result when relevant.
For invisible forces such as wind, ask for visible force cues and object response, not literal contact.
Suggested weights: interaction/contact/coordination 0.30-0.45, response/result 0.25-0.35, temporal order/causality 0.15-0.25, participant continuity 0.05-0.15.
